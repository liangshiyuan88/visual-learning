The F1 steering wheel
What does an F1 driver have in their hands? Press the steps, or wait and the wheel runs them by itself.×3
gear5DRSclosedpit limiteroffcontrols · typical20–25mass · commonly cited≈1.3 kgparts1 wheel
The big idea: a modern F1 wheel is a small computer you hold with both hands: a display and about 20–25 controls (typical), the gears on two paddles behind the grips, and a quick-release hub so it comes off the column and the driver can get out. Built mostly of carbon fibre, it weighs about 1.3 kg (commonly cited).
