核心原理： the Meta Ray-Ban 显示眼镜 keeps its screen inside the 右侧镜头. A projector in the temple pushes light into a thin glass layer in the lens, 时松开， layer steers it out toward your eye (layout typical). You never touch the glasses: the band 开启 your wrist reads the electrical signals of your muscles (肌电), so a small pinch or swipe is enough.
