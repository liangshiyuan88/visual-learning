The MacBook
Does the trackpad on a MacBook really click? Press the steps, or wait and the machine runs them by itself.×3

The big idea: your MacBook trackpad has never clicked. The glass moves 0 mm down: 4 sensors read how hard you press and a magnet under it jolts sideways, and that jolt is the click you feel. And there are 0 fans: the chip's heat spreads into the aluminium, so the whole case is the cooler. (The 52.6 Wh battery is typical for a 13-inch Air; up to 18 hours is Apple's figure.)
