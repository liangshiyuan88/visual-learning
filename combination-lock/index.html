Fig. 39 · Lock
The combination lock
Why right three turns, then left, then right? Press the steps, or wait and the lock runs them by itself.×3
dial0turn–gates lined up0 of 3wheels moving–combinations · arithmetic40 × 40 × 40 = 64,000shacklelocked
The big idea: the dial turns only wheel 1; each wheel's pin picks up the next after a full turn. So right 3, left past the 2nd once, right to the 3rd (the standard way to open one) sets the far wheel, the middle, then the near one, and lines up 3 gates under the fence. 40 numbers, 3 in the code: 40 × 40 × 40 = 64,000 combinations (arithmetic). Inner layout typical; the card's 24 · 6 · 21 is an example.
