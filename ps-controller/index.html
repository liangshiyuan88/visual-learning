The PlayStation controller
How can a trigger push back on your finger? Press the steps, or wait and the machine runs them by itself.×3
light baroffbutton–trigger push, illustrative0.0 / 10haptic actuators2battery, typical1,560 mAhparts1 controller
The big idea: the trigger fights back: behind each trigger of the DualSense a tiny motor turns a worm and a few gears that push a lever against your finger, so a bowstring feels tight and a jammed gun feels stuck (Sony calls these adaptive triggers). And the rumble is no longer a spinning weight but 2 voice coils, small speakers you feel instead of hear.
