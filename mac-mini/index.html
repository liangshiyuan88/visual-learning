The Mac mini
Where is the power button on a Mac mini? Press the steps, or wait and the machine runs them by itself.×3
size · Apple12.7 × 12.7 × 5 cmpower button · Appleunderneathstatus lightoffpower supply · Applebuilt infans · typical1parts1 box
The big idea: the Mac mini's power button is underneath, near the back-left corner, so you tip the box or reach under it to press it. The air comes in through the gap around the round foot and leaves through the back, pulled by 1 blower fan (typical), and the power supply is built in: no brick on the cable. (About 12.7 × 12.7 × 5 cm is Apple's figure; the parts inside are drawn as typical.)
