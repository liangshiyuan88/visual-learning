Fig. 33 · Satellite dish
The Starlink dish
How does a flat dish that never moves keep hold of satellites racing across the sky? Press the steps, or wait and the dish runs them by itself.×3
orbit · SpaceX≈550 kmmoving parts · SpaceX0 to steerbeam tilt–beam on–elements · typicalhundredsheater · SpaceXoffparts1 dish
The big idea: the dish never turns. Hundreds of tiny antennas under its face fire with small timing offsets, and the offsets lean the beam across the sky to follow a satellite ≈550 km up, then hand over to the next one. It even heats itself to melt snow. (All three facts are SpaceX's; the inside layout is drawn as typical.)
