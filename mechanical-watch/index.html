The mechanical watch
How does one coiled spring keep a watch ticking for a whole day? Press the steps, or wait and the machine runs them by itself.×3
beats/hour28,800beats today–power reserve16.0 hseconds wheel–jewels21parts1 watch
The big idea: a watch is a spring let go one tiny step at a time. A balance wheel swinging 4 times a second lets the gears move 8 small steps every second, 691,200 a day, and one full wind of the crown keeps it going about 40 hours (typical figures for a modern hand-wound movement).
