The RTX 5090
Where does a GeForce RTX 5090 put 575 W of heat? Press the steps, or wait and the machine runs them by itself.×3

The big idea: the board inside a GeForce RTX 5090 is a small slab in the middle of the card: the GB202 chip with its 92,000,000,000 transistors, ringed by 16 memory chips. Everything else is cooler, and both fans blow straight through it and out the back. (Transistors, 21,760 CUDA cores, 32 GB of GDDR7 and 575 W are NVIDIA's figures.)
