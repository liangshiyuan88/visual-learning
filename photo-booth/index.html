The photo booth
Where do the colours in a photo strip come from? Press the steps, or wait and the machine runs them by itself.×3

The big idea: there's no ink in a photo booth printer. A line of tiny heaters turns dye on a ribbon into gas that soaks into the paper: yellow, magenta, cyan and a clear coat, 4 passes of 1,080,000 dots each.
