The DJI Neo
How does a DJI Neo lift off an open palm and hang there at the same height with nobody flying it? Press the steps, or wait and it runs them by itself.×3
gap · sensor–props4 in guardsweight · DJIabout 135 gQuickShots · DJI4 pathsparts1 drone
The big idea: a DJI Neo takes off from and lands on an open palm, and its 4 props spin inside full guards (DJI). A sensor under its nose reads the gap to whatever is below it (layout typical), and the props speed up or ease off to hold that gap. All of it weighs about 135 g · DJI.
