The microwave
Why does a microwave have a turntable? Press the steps, or wait and the machine runs them by itself.×3
power0 Wfrequency2.45 GHzwavelength12.24 cmspot gap–speed of light–water20 °Ctimer0:00
The big idea: the waves bounce between the metal walls and stack into fixed hot spots 6.12 cm apart. Melt a still chocolate bar, double the gap and multiply by 2.45 GHz: 299,900 km/s, the speed of light. The turntable carries the food through the spots.
